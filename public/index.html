CALM RESULTS / TOWER PHYSICS · 02 OCT 2026

  1. Extract the entire ZIP into its own folder.
  2. Windows: launch Blocker.exe. Mac: open Blocker.app.
  3. Choose TOWER and build something.

Mouse + keyboard and controller supported. Local records work offline. Optional online scores use an anonymous device identity and a chosen name. F1 hides the full interface and cursor block for screenshots. Take a shelf crate with E and stack it as a step. Placed crates turn upside down so their closed bottom is the climbing surface. Crates cannot support wooden blocks. Hold Shift to walk faster. Auto text contrast waits for sustained lighting changes and fades gently between light and dark. The rear doorway reveals a small, non-enterable stairwell. When all 100 blocks are in one settled tower, the round ends automatically. At empty supply, blue outlines help you find remaining loose blocks. Edge stacks settle more calmly and the panorama seam is corrected. Collapse playback waits for the falling parts to settle, with gentle slow motion and a Fast Forward button (Space / Enter / controller A). Wood changes apply consistently to existing blocks and result towers. Live Height is the current construction, Stable Best is its confirmed peak, and Object Height refers to the comparison object. Step-up hints follow your eye level. The result screen keeps your tower in view, with This Run on the left and Your Best beside the compact leaderboard. Retry starts a fresh round. Tower autosaves on exit; choose Continue to return to your build. Wood, wall and floor finishes are in Settings > World. Continued rounds keep local records; new online scores currently require a fresh round.
