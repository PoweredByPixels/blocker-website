A quiet room. Warm wood. Real gravity.

Stack simple wooden blocks into something taller than you thought possible. Take your time. Find the balance. And when it all comes down, enjoy the moment.

MAGNETIC POLISH · 04 OCT 2026

  1. Extract the entire ZIP into its own folder.
  2. Windows: launch Blocker.exe. Mac: open Blocker.app.
  3. Choose a building world from the main menu (Mac: TOWER).

Windows modes: Creative — choose the Triumphal Arch, Cologne Cathedral or Eiffel Tower; G shows/hides the silhouette, B changes it, and Escape → Done rates coverage and building outside the guide. Wooden Railway — hold V or middle mouse, move toward a piece and release to select. Controller: hold LT, choose with the right stick and release LT. All pieces are directly accessible; center release keeps the current piece. Tab also cycles short, standard and long straights (7.5 / 15 / 30 cm), 45° curves, ramps and left/right switches. R or wheel selects one of eight starting rotations; C changes the attaching end. Hold the right mouse button or controller right-stick button to pull the connected track, K sets an aimed switch, F removes/reuses a piece and T starts/pauses the little train. Matching same-gender ends receive a coloured 2 cm adapter automatically. Each rail remains a physical piece with a visible gap and about 5° of angular play. Magnetic Tiles — the same divided part wheel with 3D previews or Tab chooses a square, equilateral triangle or tall/wide isosceles triangle. Wheel selects an edge, R folds in 90° steps, Shift + wheel folds in 5° steps, click connects and F removes. Small magnetic loads hold; unsupported arms fold under gravity and overloaded links can release. Floor tiles can connect flat along their open edges. Thick 15 mm rims at a 15 cm square edge, transparent acrylic faces, subtle handling scratches, internal ribs and embedded edge magnets catch the light. Sunlight through a tile colours the floor using a restrained dynamic transmission approximation. Flexible or unequal-edge matches are marked amber. Each world saves separately on exit; Continue returns to that world. The combined world is still to come.

Mouse + keyboard and controller supported. Local records work offline. Optional online scores use an anonymous device identity and a chosen name. F1 hides the full interface and cursor block for screenshots. Q / Y lowers or raises your view through Crawl, Crouch, Stand and Reach, one crate height per step. F picks up or swaps a wooden block. E has no gameplay action. Crate pickup is paused while these height modes are tried. R changes orientation and clears placement offsets. Hold Shift to walk faster. Auto text contrast waits for sustained lighting changes and fades gently between light and dark. The rear doorway reveals a small, non-enterable stairwell. When all 100 blocks are in one settled tower, the round ends automatically. At empty supply, blue outlines help you find remaining loose blocks. Edge stacks settle more calmly and the panorama seam is corrected. Collapse playback waits for the falling parts to settle, with gentle slow motion and a Fast Forward button (Space / Enter / controller A). Wood changes apply consistently to existing blocks and result towers. Live Height is the current construction, Stable Best is its confirmed peak, and Object Height refers to the comparison object. Height hints follow your eye level, and Continue restores your selected stance. The result screen keeps your tower in view, with This Run on the left and Your Best beside the compact leaderboard. Retry starts a fresh round. Hover over your main-menu username or pencil for a highlight; click to edit your name. Tower autosaves on exit; choose Continue to return to your build. Wood, wall and floor finishes are in Settings > World. Continued rounds keep local records; new online scores currently require a fresh round.
